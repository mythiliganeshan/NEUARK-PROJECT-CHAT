# Peaceful Garden audio

- `garden-birds.ogg`: Birds recording by kvgarlic, edited by Porrumentzio.
- `garden-wind.ogg`: Wind recording by felix.blume, edited by Porrumentzio.
- Both recordings are listed as CC0 in [Blanket's sound licensing list](https://github.com/rafaelmardojai/blanket/blob/master/SOUNDS_LICENSING.md).
- Upstream audio assets: [birds.ogg](https://github.com/rafaelmardojai/blanket/blob/master/data/resources/sounds/birds.ogg) and [wind.ogg](https://github.com/rafaelmardojai/blanket/blob/master/data/resources/sounds/wind.ogg).

The website loops these recordings at a user-adjustable volume and fades them in and out. The source files are included locally so garden playback does not require an audio hosting service.
